The Material

Woven from the finest Egyptian cotton.

Every Ducks Unlimited garment begins with long-staple Egyptian cotton — softer to the touch, stronger in the seam, and built to outlast trend cycles. We weave the cloth ourselves so we can guarantee what's inside each piece.

Our Story

Proudly made in the Philippines.

Ducks Unlimited is run by the Kuo family. Our founder, William Kuo, started out selling golf apparel just as golf was catching on in the Philippines.

From golf polos we grew into everyday clothes for men, women, teens and kids, all made locally by our sister company, CMT Manufacturing, from Egyptian cotton thread. The idea is the same as day one: international quality at local prices. Every piece you buy supports Filipino makers.
