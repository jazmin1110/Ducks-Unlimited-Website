Customer Service

Returns & Exchanges

For Ducks Unlimited and Rocwood. Both brands share one shop, one account and the same policies.

30 days to return or exchange. We want you to love what you ordered — if you don't, send it back or swap it in store.

What's eligible

Anything unworn, unwashed, with original tags attached, within 30 days of delivery. Items must be in resellable condition.

Not eligible: garments that have been worn, washed, altered, or had their tags removed.

Exchange in any of our stores

Bought online? You can exchange your item at any of our stores instead of shipping it back — no shipping cost. Bring the item with its tags attached and your order number (the confirmation email or My Orders page is fine), within 30 days of delivery.

How to return or exchange by mail

  1. Email ducksunlimited777@yahoo.com with your order number and what you'd like to return or exchange.
  2. We'll reply within one business day with a return reference and the address to ship to.
  3. Pack the items in their original packaging with the return reference written clearly on the outside.
  4. Ship via your preferred courier. We recommend something with tracking.
  5. Once we receive and inspect the items (usually within 3 business days), we send your exchange or issue your refund to your original payment method.

Shipping costs

The buyer shoulders the shipping cost of returns and exchanges — both sending the item back to us and, for exchanges, sending the new item to you. The only exception is a defective or wrong item (see below).

Exchanges

Need a different size or color? If the new piece costs more, you pay the difference (in store, or by a payment link we send you). If the new piece costs less, the difference is not refunded.

Defective or wrong item

If anything arrives damaged or different from what you ordered, email us within 7 days. We'll send a replacement and cover the shipping both ways.

Refund timing

Refunds take 7–10 business days to reach you, for every payment method, counted from when we finish inspecting your return.

Questions about a return in progress? Email ducksunlimited777@yahoo.com with your order number.
