Customer Service

Shipping

For Ducks Unlimited and Rocwood. Both brands share one shop, one account and the same policies.

We ship anywhere in the Philippines. Free shipping on orders of ₱3,000 and up — or pick up free in any of our stores.

Rates & Times

  • Metro Manila: ₱100, arrives in 1–3 days
  • Luzon (outside Metro Manila): ₱150, arrives in 2–5 days
  • Visayas and Mindanao: ₱200, arrives in 3–7 days
  • Orders of ₱3,000 and up: free shipping, anywhere in the Philippines
  • Store pickup: free

The fee is worked out from your province and shown at checkout before you pay. Orders ship from Greenhills, San Juan through our courier partners.

Ways to pay

Pay online by card, Maya, GCash or QR Ph, or choose cash on delivery and pay the courier when your order arrives.

Processing Times

Standard orders are packed within 1 business day. During sales or holidays, give us up to 2 business days. You'll get an email when your order ships, with tracking information.

Tracking

Once your order ships, the tracking number is added to your order page in My Orders and sent by email. Tracking links open with the relevant courier.

International

Right now we only ship within the Philippines. For international requests, email us — we can arrange forwarding to a Philippine address you choose.

Pickup

You can also pick up your order for free at one of our stores. Choose "Pick up in store" at checkout and pick the store. Orders are usually ready in 1–2 days, and we'll email you when yours is ready.
