Product Images

Up to 12 photos. JPG, PNG, or WEBP — max 5 MB each. You can pick several at once. The first photo is the main one customers see — tap Make main on any photo (or drag photos on a computer) to change the order. Use the dropdown on each photo to say which color it shows — when a shopper picks that color, its photos appear. Leave it on All colors for photos that suit every color.

Variants

A variant is one color + size, e.g. "Navy, M". Each one gets its own stock count (online and Greenhills store), starting at 0 — set the stock on the Inventory page.

Add a color in many sizes

Type the color, tap the sizes you have, then press Add. SKU codes are made for you.
